## Verdandi

**Procedural game audio · Rust · Bevy 0.20**

A seed-driven music engine that generates evolving, layered soundtracks at runtime. Choose between four musical identities, shape the arrangement with intensity and layer controls, and trigger musical cues from gameplay—without bundling prerecorded tracks.

[Project page]({{ '/verdandi/' | relative_url }}) · [Docs & API guides]({{ '/verdandi/docs/' | relative_url }}) · [View repository](https://github.com/right-ward/verdandi)
